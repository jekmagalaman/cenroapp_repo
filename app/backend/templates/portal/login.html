Bantay Dagat Program

Bantay Dagat is a program under the Environmental Law Enforcement Division of the Office of the City ENRO tasked by the City Government of Puerto Princesa to coordinate with other Government Agencies in charge in the implementation of Fishery laws and policies within the City Waters. In relation to such functions, this program is authorized to conduct operations towards the apprehension of illegal acts, and conservation, development, protection, utilization and disposition of all fisheries and aquatic resources within the City Waters.
